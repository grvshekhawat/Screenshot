When uploading screenshots to App Store Connect, encountering errors can be frustrating. These errors often stem from incorrect dimensions, file formats, or device misalignment. Here’s how to troubleshoot and fix these issues efficiently.

### Common Screenshot Upload Errors
1. **Incorrect Dimensions**: Ensure your screenshots meet the required dimensions for each device. For specific sizes, refer to our [App Store Screenshot Sizes guide](/blog/app-store-screenshot-sizes).

2. **Wrong File Format**: Screenshots should be in PNG or JPEG format. Double-check the format before uploading.

3. **Device Misalignment**: Screenshots must match the device type you’re targeting. For instance, iPhone screenshots should be formatted correctly for the specific model, such as the [iPhone 6.9 vs 6.5 App Store Screenshots](/blog/iphone-69-vs-65-app-store-screenshots).
