Once your slides look right, exporting cleanly is the last mile. Screenshot Studio separates **preview** (free) from **store-ready** (Pro) so you can iterate without paying until you ship.

## Free vs Pro

| Export | Free | Pro |
|--------|------|-----|
| Single-slide PNG | Watermarked | Clean |
| ZIP for current size | — | Yes |
| ZIP for all sizes (same orientation) | — | Yes |

## Steps

1. Sign in and open a project under [/app](/app).
2. Finish copy, frames, and clipart on each slide.
3. Open **Export** in the left rail.
4. Free users download a watermarked preview. Pro users choose PNG or ZIP.
5. Upload the files in App Store Connect or Play Console.

## Orientation reminder

Portrait and landscape projects are separate. Exporting “all sizes” only covers store sizes that match the project orientation—exactly what Apple and Google expect for each set.
