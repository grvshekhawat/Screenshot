Creating the perfect app store screenshots is crucial for attracting users and boosting downloads. With frequent updates to the App Store and Google Play, it's important to stay informed about the latest screenshot size requirements.

## App Store Screenshot Sizes

For the App Store, screenshots must adhere to specific dimensions based on the device type:

- **iPhone 6.5"**: 1242 x 2688 pixels
- **iPhone 5.5"**: 1242 x 2208 pixels
- **iPad Pro (12.9")**: 2048 x 2732 pixels

For more detailed guidance, check out our [App Store screenshot sizes guide](/blog/app-store-screenshot-sizes).

## Google Play Screenshot Sizes

Google Play offers more flexibility, but there are still recommended sizes to consider:

- **Minimum**: 320 x 320 pixels
- **Maximum**: 3840 x 3840 pixels

For optimal results, it's best to provide screenshots that cover a range of devices. Explore our [Google Play screenshot sizes guide](/blog/google-play-screenshot-sizes) for more tips.
