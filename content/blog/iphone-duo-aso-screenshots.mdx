App Store Optimization (ASO) is still how most people discover and decide to install your app. Keywords get you into search results; **screenshots and preview creatives** close the tap. When new device shapes show up—like foldable **iPhone Duo** poses—the listings that look current and intentional convert better than ones stuck on a single phone chrome.

## Why ASO creative quality matters

- **Search is crowded.** Ranking for a keyword is only half the job. Shoppers swipe your screenshot carousel before they read the description.
- **First three frames do most of the work.** Lead with the clearest value prop, then proof, then detail—same story on every size you ship.
- **Device chrome signals polish.** Native-looking frames (not stretched PNGs) make the product feel real. Outdated or mismatched chrome quietly tanks trust.
- **New form factors create contrast.** When Duo (or any foldable) appears in browse and ads, listings that already show open and closed poses look ahead of the pack.

ASO is not a one-time keyword pass. Treat screenshot sets like product packaging you refresh when the device story changes.

## What iPhone Duo changes for screenshots

Duo is a **pose story**, not only a new pixel size:

- **Closed** — cover-style frame (hinge on the left, punch camera). Good for glanceable UI and “fits in your pocket” messaging.
- **Open** / **Open landscape** — wide inner display. Use it when your app’s value is multitasking, canvas, reading, or side-by-side layouts.

You do not need a separate brand for Duo. Keep the same headline system and screenshot library; swap the device frame so the listing matches how people will hold the product.

## Practical ASO checklist for Duo-ready listings

1. **Lead with benefit, not chrome.** The device should support the story, not replace it.
2. **Show at least one closed and one open frame** if your app gains from the fold (or clearly works on both).
3. **Keep text in safe areas** on every export size—foldables do not forgive edge-cropped CTAs.
4. **Match localization.** Same language as the listing locale; do not ship English-only creatives into other storefronts.
5. **Export every required iPhone class** for that orientation—Duo poses sit alongside your normal 6.9" / 6.5" set, not instead of it.

## Ship Duo frames in Screenshot Studio

In [Screenshot Studio](/login):

1. Create a **Portrait** or **Landscape** project (orientation is locked per project).
2. Add a phone and pick **iPhone Duo · Closed** or **iPhone Duo · Open** in Properties → Device.
3. Drop in the same screens you already use for iPhone—or dedicated wide layouts for open.
4. Spot-check scale, tilt, and headlines on each store size.
5. On Pro, export a **ZIP for all sizes** so your Duo-ready set lands with the rest of the listing assets.
