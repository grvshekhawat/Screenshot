Google Play asks for phone screenshots (and often a feature graphic) before you can publish. Getting dimensions right the first time avoids Console errors and last-minute redesigns.

## Phone screenshots

Play expects vertical or horizontal phone screenshots within the size ranges listed in Play Console for your release track. In practice:

- Design on a modern phone canvas (Screenshot Studio’s Play phone target).
- Export lossless PNG when you can.
- Keep captions short and high-contrast so they stay readable on the store listing.

## Feature graphic

The Play **feature graphic** is a wide promotional banner (commonly 1024×500). It is separate from in-app screenshot frames—treat it as a marketing banner, not a phone mockup.

## Workflow

1. Create a **portrait** or **landscape** project for Play phone screenshots.
2. Use a [template](/templates) that already has headline and device placement.
3. Export a clean PNG or ZIP on [Pro](/pricing).
