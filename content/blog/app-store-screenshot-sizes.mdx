Apple still expects device-class screenshots for App Store Connect. Exact pixel sizes change over time, but the practical workflow is the same: design for your primary phone size, then export matching frames for every required display.

## What to prepare

- **iPhone** — typically the largest Super Retina sizes (for example 6.9" and 6.5" classes) plus any additional sizes App Store Connect flags for your app.
- **iPad** — 13" and 11" class displays when you ship an iPad app.
- **Orientation** — portrait and landscape are separate creatives. Do not rotate a portrait set and call it landscape.

## Fast workflow in Screenshot Studio

1. Pick **Portrait** or **Landscape** when you create the project (orientation is locked per project).
2. Start from a template on the [templates gallery](/templates).
3. Drop your app screenshots onto the device frames.
4. On Pro, export a **ZIP for all sizes** so you get every matching store size for that orientation.

## Tips that save rejection cycles

- Keep critical UI away from rounded corners and the Dynamic Island / status bar.
- Match the language of your App Store listing localization.
- Prefer crisp PNG exports; avoid heavy compression before upload.
